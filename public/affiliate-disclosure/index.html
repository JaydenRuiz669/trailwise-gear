Affiliate disclosure

Our current monetization status and intended disclosure practice.

Current status

As of October 26, 2026, Trailwise Gear has no active affiliate tracking links on this website. Manufacturer and reference links are ordinary links. We have not claimed affiliate approval or commissions.

Future affiliate links

If we join an affiliate program, we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases through clearly identified links. We will update this disclosure and place a clear disclosure near recommendations containing those links. We will also follow each program’s required wording and linking rules.

Your choices

You are free to use our guides without buying anything. We aim to recommend equipment based on fit, evidence, and total cost. Commercial relationships do not establish that a product was tested or that it is the best option for everyone.
