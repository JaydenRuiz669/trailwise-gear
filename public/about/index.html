THE PUBLICATION

About Trailwise Gear

Practical outdoor information for people who care where their money goes.

Our purpose

Trailwise Gear is an independent publication operated by Jayden Ruiz. We help budget-conscious campers and outdoor enthusiasts compare equipment, prepare for trips, and avoid buying gear that does not solve a real problem.

What you will find

Our initial publication focuses on beginner car camping, complete cooking systems, and conservative vehicle-trip preparation. The first ten guides are desk-researched. They do not represent field tests or professional assessments of your equipment.

How the business works

We may earn commissions through disclosed affiliate links, including BougeRV links in relevant guides. A commercial relationship does not mean that we tested a product. Editorial recommendations include borrowing, reusing equipment, and delaying purchases when those options fit the reader better.

Accountability

Sources and evidence limits appear in each guide. Readers can report factual errors through our contact page. See the editorial policy for our research, AI assistance, and correction practices.
