THE PUBLICATION

Editorial & review policy

How we research, label evidence, and correct mistakes.

Research-based vs hands-on

Every initial guide is desk-researched. Manufacturer statements are attributed and do not become our measured results. A future hands-on review must identify the product version, conditions, method, and limitations. We will not imply that we used a product when we have not.

Sources and recommendations

We prioritize manufacturer documentation for specifications and compatibility, public land managers for access guidance, and authoritative safety sources for safety claims. Prices and availability change; we do not publish invented prices or claim real-time stock. We compare complete systems and distinguish editorial judgment from verified facts.

AI assistance

AI tools assist with research, writing, development, and maintenance. This does not constitute hands-on product testing or independent expert certification. We check important factual claims against linked sources and do not invent customer reviews, lab results, credentials, or affiliate relationships.

Commercial independence

We plan to use affiliate links only after program approval and receipt of real tracking information. A relationship will be disclosed near relevant links. We do not sell positive conclusions. Borrowing and not buying remain legitimate recommendations. No products were supplied for the initial guides.

Corrections and updates

Report errors through the contact page. Material corrections will be recorded in the affected article, with an updated date. Dates change when substantive review occurs, not automatically to imply freshness. Safety instructions and local rules must always be checked again before use.
