How to choose

Start with an appropriately rated quilt or sleep system and a suitable insulated pad. Check manufacturer temperature guidance, mattress R-value, size, packed weight, return policy and current price. For car camping, weight may matter less than comfort; for backpacking, carry weight and volume matter more.

Don't overspend on the first trip

Before buying, compare the full sleep setup—not just a quilt or mattress by itself. If you already own suitable gear, try it on a short, manageable trip first. Check nighttime forecasts and leave a margin for colder-than-expected conditions.
